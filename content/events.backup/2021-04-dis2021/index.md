abstract: |
  Transverse single-spin asymmetry (TSSA) measurements have driven the development of both transverse-momentum-dependent and collinear twist-3 descriptions of nonperturbative spin-momentum correlations. This talk presents TSSA results for direct photons, neutral pions, and eta mesons at midrapidity (|η| < 0.35) from transversely polarized p+p collisions at √s = 200 GeV at PHENIX. As hadrons, π0 and η mesons are sensitive to both initial- and final-state effects and probe gluon dynamics along with a mix of quark flavors at midrapidity. Because direct photon production does not involve hadronization, the direct photon TSSA is sensitive only to initial-state effects, offering a clean probe of gluon dynamics in transversely polarized protons. Together, these three measurements help constrain the collinear twist-3 trigluon correlation function and the gluon Sivers function, improving understanding of spin-dependent gluon dynamics in QCD.
