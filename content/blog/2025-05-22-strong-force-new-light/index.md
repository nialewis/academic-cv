The Wiess School of Natural Sciences at Rice University highlights the experimental research of postdoctoral researcher **Dr. Nicole Lewis** with the Compact Muon Solenoid (CMS) experiment at CERN's Large Hadron Collider.

The article explores how studying ultraperipheral and photonuclear collisions—where high-energy heavy ions exchange photons rather than colliding head-on—offers a unique, clean environment to investigate the strong nuclear force and search for collective behavior and quark-gluon plasma in smaller collision systems.
