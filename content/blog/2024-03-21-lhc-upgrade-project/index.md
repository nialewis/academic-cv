A team of nuclear physicists at Rice University—including Professor Wei Li, Frank Geurts, Nicole Lewis, and Mike Matveev—has been selected by the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Office of Nuclear Physics to lead the construction of a critical detector upgrade for the Compact Muon Solenoid (CMS) experiment at CERN.

The five-year, $15.5 million project focuses on the **Endcap Timing Layer (ETL)**, an ultra-fast silicon timing detector capable of precision time-of-flight measurements (30 picoseconds per particle), crucial for disentangling collisions at the upcoming High-Luminosity LHC.
