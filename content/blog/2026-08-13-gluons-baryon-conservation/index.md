New experimental results from the STAR Collaboration at Brookhaven National Laboratory's Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC) challenge the conventional picture of how protons and other baryons maintain their identity. 

While the standard textbook model assumed that three constituent quarks carried the fundamental "baryon number," high-energy collision measurements indicate that a Y-shaped network of gluons—known as a **baryon junction**—may serve as the true carrier of baryon number during collisions.
