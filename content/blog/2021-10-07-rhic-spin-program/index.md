At the Fall Meeting of the American Physical Society Division of Nuclear Physics (APS DNP), researchers from the RHIC spin program presented breakthrough measurements on how quarks and gluons contribute to the overall spin of the proton.

The presentations showcased decades of polarized proton-proton collision results at RHIC and previewed opportunities for next-generation nuclear physics studies at the future Electron-Ion Collider (EIC).
