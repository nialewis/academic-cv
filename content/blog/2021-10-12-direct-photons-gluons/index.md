Nuclear physicists analyzing data from the PHENIX experiment at the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC) demonstrated a powerful approach to investigating the dynamic internal structure of protons using direct photons.

Because direct photons do not interact via the strong force after production, they escape collision zones cleanly, providing an unadulterated probe of gluon spin-momentum correlations inside transversely polarized protons.
